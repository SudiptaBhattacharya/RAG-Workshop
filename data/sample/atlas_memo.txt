PROJECT ATLAS — INTERNAL LAUNCH MEMO

Project Atlas will launch on 17 November 2026. The approved spending cap is AUD 42,000. Any request above this limit requires written approval from the programme sponsor.

The pilot will begin with 120 postgraduate students. The success criteria are: at least 80 percent task completion, a median response time below four seconds, and zero disclosure of private student records.

The team will review the pilot after six weeks. Questions about procurement should be directed to the programme office.
